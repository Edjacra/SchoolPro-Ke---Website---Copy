Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

SchoolPro Ke is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of student, parent, teacher, and school administrative data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share information when you visit our website or use our platform.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information that identifies or can be used to identify a person, including:

  • School contact details and administrator information
  • Student names, student numbers, class, stream, parent/guardian information, and academic records
  • Fee payment and financial transaction data
  • Attendance, timetable, and communication records
  • Login information, account activity, and support communications
  • Technical information such as browser type, IP address, device identifiers, and usage trends

We collect this information directly from the school, authorized staff, parents, and from our platform’s normal operation. We do not sell personal data.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Operate the SchoolPro Ke platform and provide core school administration services
  • Manage admissions, fee collection, attendance, academic records, report cards, and communication
  • Support school operations such as timetable planning, notices, bulk messaging, and reporting
  • Improve user experience, system reliability, and service quality
  • Respond to inquiries, support requests, security issues, and operational needs
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations applicable to school data processing

3. Data sharing and disclosures

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With authorized school staff and designated administrators acting within the school’s own policies
  • With parents or guardians where required for school administration and communication
  • With trusted third-party service providers that support hosting, security, analytics, payment processing, and technical operations, subject to confidentiality obligations
  • When required by law, court order, or legitimate regulatory enforcement
  • To protect the rights, safety, or security of the platform, our users, or the public

We do not sell personal data to marketing databases or unrelated third parties.

4. Data protection and security

We apply reasonable organisational and technical safeguards to protect personal data, including access controls, secure hosting, encrypted data transmission, user authentication, and backup procedures. However, no system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection against all risks.

5. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide services to the school, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymise it in line with our retention practices.

6. Your rights

Depending on the applicable law and the school’s internal policies, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data, and to ask questions about how your information is used. Schools and institutions remain responsible for informing staff and parents about their data rights within their own policies and lawful processing framework.

7. Cookies and website analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve functionality, remember preferences, and understand website usage. These tools may collect anonymised or pseudonymised usage information. Users may disable cookies in their browser settings, although some features may not work as intended.

8. Children’s and student data

SchoolPro Ke may process student data as part of the school’s lawful educational operations. We treat student data as highly sensitive and limit access to authorized personnel only. We do not use student data for unrelated marketing or profiling activities.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services evolve or as applicable laws change. Any material changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of personal data, please contact us at schoolproke@gmail.com or use the contact details on our website.
