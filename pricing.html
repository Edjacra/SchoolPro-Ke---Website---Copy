Pricing questions

How much does SchoolPro Ke cost?

Pricing depends on the number of students enrolled at your school, so it stays affordable whether you're a small or a large school. Contact us by call, WhatsApp or email and we'll give you a quote for your student numbers.

Is there a setup or onboarding fee?

Onboarding, data migration guidance and staff training are included — ask us for the details when you contact us.

Do parents pay to use the parent portal?

No — the parent portal and SMS notices are part of the platform at no extra cost to parents.

Can our price change later?

Since pricing is based on student numbers, it's reviewed as your enrolment changes. Reach out any time and we'll walk you through it.
