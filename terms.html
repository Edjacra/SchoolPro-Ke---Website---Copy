Terms of Service

Transparent commitments to system availability, data protection under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, 100% institutional data ownership, and enterprise-grade platform performance.

Built on Trust, High Uptime & Statutory Compliance

SchoolPro Ke is designed to be your long-term technology partner. We guarantee 100% data ownership for your school, transparent SLA support, high system uptime during critical term seasons, and strict adherence to Kenyan laws.

1

Statutory Compliance & Regulatory Standard

SchoolPro Ke is bound by and operates under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. We adhere strictly to statutory provisions governing data processing, confidentiality, and institutional data handling in Kenya.

2

100% School Data Ownership & Integrity

Your educational institution retains absolute 100% ownership of all student registers, academic CBC marks, attendance records, and financial fee logs. SchoolPro Ke acts purely as a technology provider and custodian. Your data belongs exclusively to your institution, and you may request full data exports at any time without restriction.

3

Service Level Commitment & High Availability

We understand that school operations depend on steady, uninterrupted access — especially during admission drives, M-Pesa fee collection periods, and CBC report card compilation. We commit to maintaining high platform uptime, enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure, proactive load balancing, and prompt technical support for all subscribed schools.

4

Platform Usage & Authorized Staff Accounts

Schools may create unlimited authorized accounts for principals, registrars, accountants, teachers, and librarians. Each staff member receives secure credentials managed via Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to ensure security and administrative oversight across all streams and departments.

5

Transparent Subscription & Predictable Pricing

Our pricing is simple, transparent, and scaled directly to your student enrolment size. There are no hidden fees or locked feature modules. Subscription renewals are clearly communicated, ensuring predictable budgeting for every school term.

6

Continuous System Updates & Security Enhancements

We continuously upgrade SchoolPro Ke to align with national educational changes (such as CBC updates, KNEC guidelines, and Ministry of Education reporting requirements) and security enhancements — provided to all active subscriber schools at no extra charge.

7

Dedicated Customer Support & Onboarding

Every school on SchoolPro Ke receives dedicated onboarding, staff training, and ongoing technical support via direct phone, WhatsApp, and email assistance to ensure smooth daily administration.
